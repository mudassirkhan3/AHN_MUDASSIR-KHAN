Unit I • Orthopedics 🔥 High-Frequency

Compartment Syndrome & RICE Protocol

Compartment Syndrome: Elevated tissue pressure within a non-yielding myofascial compartment compromising neurovascular function.

⚡ The 6 Ps (Must-Know Viva List):

Pain (out of proportion), Paresthesia, Pallor, Paralysis, Pulselessness, Poikilothermia.

💡 Key Nursing Action: Emergency Fasciotomy required. Do NOT elevate or apply ice to an acute compartment syndrome limb.
Unit II • Endocrine 🔥 High-Frequency

Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA) & Hypocalcemia Signs

DKA Definition: Acute life-threatening metabolic triad of Hyperglycemia (>250 mg/dL), Metabolic Acidosis (pH <7.30, HCO3 <18), and Ketonuria secondary to absolute insulin deficiency.

Trousseau's Sign

Carpopedal spasm induced by inflating BP cuff above systolic pressure for 3 mins (Hypocalcemia).

Chvostek's Sign

Facial twitching triggered by tapping over the facial nerve anterior to the ear lobe.

Unit III • Neurology 🔥 High-Frequency

Cushing's Triad & Guillain-Barré Syndrome

Normal ICP Range: 5 to 15 mmHg. Treatment is required if sustained >20 mmHg.

⚠️ Cushing's Triad (Late Sign of Increased ICP):
  • Systolic Hypertension with Widened Pulse Pressure
  • Bradycardia (slow heart rate)
  • Bradypnea / Irregular respirations (Cheyne-Stokes)

Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS): Post-infectious autoimmune polyneuropathy causing progressive, ascending symmetrical motor weakness and loss of deep tendon reflexes.

Unit IV • Cardiovascular

Myocardial Infarction & Virchow's Triad

Myocardial Infarction (MI): Irreversible myocardial necrosis caused by prolonged ischemia. Troponin I & T are the most specific cardiac biomarkers.

Virchow's Triad (DVT Pathogenesis):

1. Stasis of blood flow  •  2. Endothelial injury  •  3. Hypercoagulability

Unit V • Pulmonary

ARDS & Refractory Hypoxemia

Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS): Severe non-cardiogenic inflammatory lung injury resulting in sudden, diffuse alveolar damage and massive fluid accumulation.

⚡ Hallmark Viva Answer: Refractory Hypoxemia — Arterial hypoxemia that does not improve even when supplemental 100% oxygen is administered.
Unit VI • Eye & ENT

Meniere's Disease & Retinal Detachment

Retinal Detachment: Separation of the sensory retina from the underlying retinal pigment epithelium. Patients describe a "dark curtain or shade coming down over vision."

Meniere's Disease Classic Triad:

1. Episodic Vertigo  •  2. Sensorineural Hearing Loss  •  3. Tinnitus (ringing in ears)

Unit VII • Burns 🔥 High-Frequency

Parkland Formula & Rule of Nines

📐 Parkland Fluid Formula:
4 mL × Body Weight (kg) × % TBSA Burned = Total 24h Lactated Ringer's
  • • First 8 Hours: Give 50% of the total calculated fluid.
  • • Next 16 Hours: Give remaining 50% of total fluid.
